Our story

Caring today, healing tomorrow — for every Kenyan family

TopZen Healthcare Limited was founded to make genuine, affordable healthcare products accessible to every home — backed by licensed pharmacists and honest service.

Why we exist

Healthcare shouldn't be a gamble

Too many people buy medicine without knowing where it came from or whether it's genuine. TopZen was built to close that gap: every product we stock is sourced from licensed manufacturers and distributors, verified by our pharmacy team, and delivered with the same care you'd expect from a trusted neighborhood chemist — just faster, and online.

From everyday pain relief to home medical equipment for a recovering relative, we treat every order like it matters, because it does.

What guides us

Our values

Reliability

Milestones

How we got here

2022 — The idea

Founded with a simple mission: bring trustworthy pharmacy service online for Kenyan families.

2023 — First deliveries

Launched delivery across Nairobi with a small catalog of essential medicines and PPE.

2024 — Growing the catalog

Expanded into medical equipment, wellness products, and mother & baby care.

2026 — Serving thousands

Now delivering nationwide, with a growing team of licensed pharmacists supporting every order.
